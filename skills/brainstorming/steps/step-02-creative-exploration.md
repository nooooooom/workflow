# Step 2: Creative Exploration

**Goal:** Use structured creative techniques to generate diverse ideas and explore the solution space.

## Execution Sequence

1. **Technique Selection**

   Choose appropriate brainstorming technique(s) based on the problem type:

   - **First Principles Thinking** - Break down to fundamental truths and build up
   - **SCAMPER** - Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other uses, Eliminate, Reverse
   - **Six Thinking Hats** - Different perspectives (facts, emotions, caution, benefits, creativity, process)
   - **Mind Mapping** - Visual exploration of connected concepts
   - **Reverse Brainstorming** - How to cause the problem, then invert
   - **Analogy Thinking** - "How would [other domain] solve this?"
   - **Worst Possible Idea** - Generate terrible ideas to remove inhibition
   - **Constraint Removal** - What if we had unlimited resources/time?

2. **Apply Selected Techniques**

   For each technique:

   a. **Generate Ideas (Quantity Phase)**
      - Produce many ideas without judgment
      - Aim for 20-30 ideas minimum per technique
      - Document all ideas, even seemingly bad ones
      - Consciously shift creative domains every 10 ideas to avoid clustering

   b. **Explore Promising Directions**
      - Identify 3-5 most promising idea clusters
      - Dig deeper into each with follow-up questions
      - Look for unexpected combinations

   c. **Document Insights**
      - Key ideas generated
      - Surprising connections discovered
      - Constraints or requirements revealed

3. **Clarifying Questions**

   Based on creative exploration, ask clarifying questions one at a time:

   - Purpose: What problem are we solving?
   - Users: Who will use this?
   - Constraints: What limitations exist?
   - Success: How do we measure success?
   - Context: Where does this fit in the larger system?

   **Rules:**
   - One question per message
   - Multiple choice preferred when possible
   - Open-ended is fine when needed
   - Focus on understanding, not leading

4. **Transition to Design**

   When you have:
   - ✅ Explored creative possibilities
   - ✅ Understood the problem space
   - ✅ Identified key constraints and requirements

   Read fully and follow: `./step-03-approach-proposal.md`

## Creative Technique Details

### First Principles Thinking
1. Identify the problem
2. Break it down to fundamental truths
3. Question assumptions
4. Reconstruct from basics

### SCAMPER Method
- **Substitute**: What can be replaced?
- **Combine**: What can be merged?
- **Adapt**: What else is like this?
- **Modify**: What can be changed?
- **Put to other uses**: New applications?
- **Eliminate**: What's unnecessary?
- **Reverse**: What if we do the opposite?

### Six Thinking Hats
- **White Hat**: Facts and data
- **Red Hat**: Emotions and feelings
- **Black Hat**: Risks and problems
- **Yellow Hat**: Benefits and value
- **Green Hat**: Creativity and alternatives
- **Blue Hat**: Process and organization

## Anti-Bias Reminder

Consciously shift creative domains:
- Ideas 1-10: Technical aspects

- Ideas 21-30: Business viability
- Ideas 31-40: Edge cases and risks
- Ideas 41-50: Novel combinations
